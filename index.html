Canva Review

Canva is useful for creating creative teaching materials such as posters, presentations, infographics, worksheets, and classroom visuals.

Kahoot Review

Kahoot uses game-based learning to make quizzes exciting. It can increase motivation, encourage participation, and make revision fun.

Padlet Review

Padlet works like a digital wall where students can share ideas, upload images, answer questions, and collaborate with classmates.

Quizizz Review

Quizizz allows teachers to create interactive quizzes that students can answer at their own pace. It is suitable for formative assessment and revision.

Google Classroom

Google Classroom helps organize classwork, assignments, announcements, and learning resources in a simple online environment.

My Review

Most Creative

Canva is the best for designing creative and attractive learning materials.

Most Fun

Kahoot and Quizizz make learning feel like a game, which can motivate students.

Best Management

Google Classroom is useful for organizing assignments and learning resources.
