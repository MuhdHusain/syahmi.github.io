Canva

Canva is useful for creating creative teaching materials such as posters, presentations, infographics, worksheets, and classroom visuals. It helps teachers make lessons more attractive and easier for students to understand.

Kahoot

Kahoot uses game-based learning to make quizzes more exciting. It can increase student motivation, encourage participation, and make revision sessions more fun.

Padlet

Padlet works like a digital wall where students can share ideas, upload images, answer questions, and collaborate with classmates. It is very useful for brainstorming and discussion activities.

Quizizz

Quizizz allows teachers to create interactive quizzes that students can answer at their own pace. It is suitable for formative assessment, revision, and checking student understanding.

Google Classroom

Google Classroom helps organize classwork, assignments, announcements, and learning resources. It supports communication between teachers and students in a simple online learning environment.

My Review

Most Creative

Canva is the best for designing creative and attractive learning materials.

Most Fun

Kahoot and Quizizz make learning feel like a game, which can motivate students.

Best for Classroom Management

Google Classroom is very useful for organizing assignments and learning resources.
